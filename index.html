Preparing the Diwali calendar

A little guide

Light the way to Diwali

Each diya holds a small festival moment. Follow the glow and let the calendar unfold at its own pace.

  • ✦

    Choose a lamp to uncover a daily surprise, story, or festive discovery.

  • ✦

    Light them in order and watch more of the mandala emerge around the calendar.

  • ✦

    Keep an eye on the cards: a playful emoji can point to an extra hidden moment.

  • ✦

    Notice the moon above as its phase changes with your journey through the days.
