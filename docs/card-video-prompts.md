# Diwali video prompts

Generate each film in vertical 9:16 (1080×1920), 8–12 seconds, with no text,
logos, watermarks, or audio baked in. The styles intentionally vary between
cinematic realism and illustration so the calendar feels playful rather than
visually uniform. Keep one calm, continuous camera move so the card can return
cleanly to its front when the film ends.

## Day 1 — Rows of Diyas

Save as `assets/videos/card-stories/day-01-rows-of-diyas.mp4`.

> Cinematic photorealistic vertical 9:16 Diwali film, a slow camera glide
> beside a long row of clay diyas glowing along a stone doorstep at blue hour.
> Flames flicker gently and reflect on the ground; distant warm household light
> creates soft bokeh. Calm, hopeful, intimate mood, no people, no text, no
> logos, no watermark, no sudden cuts.

## Day 15 — Floating diya (standalone lamp video)

Save as `assets/videos/card-stories/day-15-floating-diya.mp4`.

> Stylised stop-motion miniature vertical 9:16 Diwali night film, crafted clay
> figures and hand-painted water. A small clay diya with a steady golden flame
> is released onto still water beside flower petals; it glides toward other
> glowing lamps while its reflection shimmers below. Low water-level camera,
> warm amber against deep indigo, tactile handmade texture, peaceful and
> reverent Diwali atmosphere, no text, no logos, no watermark, no sudden cuts.

## Day 7 — Sparkler moment

Save as `assets/videos/card-stories/day-07-sparkler.mp4`.

> Cinematic photorealistic vertical 9:16 Diwali evening film, close-up of a

## Replacing the temporary clips

The experiment currently plays existing sample MP4s so its behaviour can be
tested. Once the generated files are present, update the matching `src` values
in `assets/diwali-experiences.js`; Day 15's source lives under `media.src`.
